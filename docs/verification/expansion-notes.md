## Scope and preserved boundaries

This is a standalone, compressed low-poly explorer. Source-linked real infrastructure informs the scene, while dimensions, equipment placement and simulation timing are authored for exploration. The port functionality is an in-memory visual simulation; it does not connect to shipping, ferry booking or private Lagos Life game services. Original-game accounts, saves, economy and NPC services remain outside this task.

`dist/`, `.openai/hosting.json` and Abuja source were verified unchanged. No paid hosting plan was purchased; no original-game deployment/data was altered. The user's explicit 2026-10-09 Workers request supersedes older local-only notes. No Git commit or push was requested or performed. Research and model limitations are in `docs/research/dublin-density-evidence.md`, `airport-expansion.md` and `roads-port-evidence.md`.
