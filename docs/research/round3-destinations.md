# Round 3 Dublin destination references

The 45 additional places are individually selectable in an authored, compressed low-poly scene. Their coordinates and parcel sizes are illustrative. Source URLs identify the counterpart; geometry emphasizes recognizable massing, roofs, frontages and industrial features. These references do not establish original-game internals or live services.

The National Stadium uses a rectangular purpose-built boxing hall rather than a circular sports bowl ([venue history](https://www.thenationalstadium.ie/thenationalstadium)). Salesforce uses four interconnected mid-rise volumes and a roof garden, following [Salesforce’s campus description](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2023/05/04/salesforce-dublin-office/). Gordon House uses a broad office block; Poolbeg has two banded stacks, while Dublin Waste to Energy has a separate process hall and vent. The Botanic Gardens glasshouse and Stephen’s Green mall use actual curved roof geometry.
